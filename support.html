Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Learn French Easy, then tap Cancel Subscription. Subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple; we don't have access to cancel them on our end.

I restored my purchase but lessons are still locked. What do I do?
Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used to subscribe, then open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases." If the issue persists, email us with your Apple ID email (not your password) and we'll help sort it out.

Is my progress saved if I get a new phone?
Lesson progress is currently stored locally on your device. Subscription status restores automatically via your Apple ID, but progress does not yet sync across devices.

How do the pronunciation hints work?
Every new word includes a plain-English guide to how it actually sounds (like "bonjour" → "bohn-ZHOOR"), so silent letters, nasal vowels, and French sounds that don't exist in English are never a guessing game.

Something isn't working / I found a bug.
Email us a quick description of what happened (and a screenshot if you have one) at amaroki2004@yahoo.com — we read every message.
